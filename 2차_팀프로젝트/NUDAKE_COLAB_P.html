2021 PICNIC


In the fall of 2021, NUDAKE launched a new collection focused on the theme of 'Dining'. This creative
lineup shakes up cake ingredients, recipes, and how we eat them. It explores different ways of
enjoying food beyond the usual meals and cakes.

CAKE

The 'Picnic' cake is the signature menu of the Dining collection, featuring seasonal fruits, to
represent the beauty of nature.

  • Flaky pastry, almond cream and cream cheese, topped with fresh seasonal fruit in an edible multi-grain bread basket

  • "Flaky pastry, almond cream and cream cheese, topped with fresh seasonal fruit in an edible multi-grain bread basket"

FAKE DOCUMENTARY FILM

We've documented the heartfelt stories behind the Picnic cake in a documentary. You can watch
the Picnic's story on our YouTube channel.

CONTEST

The 'Picnic' cake can be versatile, depending on how candles are arranged or how the ingredients
are presented. We hosted a contest to acknowledge individuals who creatively staged beautiful
Picnic cakes, rewarding them with prizes.
