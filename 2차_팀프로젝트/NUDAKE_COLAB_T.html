NUDAKE   TEAHOUSE

The NUDAKE Teahouse is a space dedicated to our pioneering tea experiences.
Here, you can savor ‘scented teas’ and carefully curated
‘dessert pairings,’ all created with audacious vision and understated sophistication.
Through NUDAKE, we hope that your senses awaken,
and the personal stories you thought forgotten quietly come to life.

TEA

At NUDAKE, we craft teas inspired by imagery beyond the ordinary,
layering scents in surprising ways to create entirely new scented teas.
At the Teahouse, explore twelve exquisite teas,
served in elegant teapots and presented in thoughtfully designed packages.

TEA GIFT

NUDAKE tea packages combine sophisticated design with thoughtfully
curated selections, offering an unforgettable sensory experience
for yourself or someone special.

SPACE

NUDAKE’s space is defined by yellow-green walls and floors accented
with purple objects, creating a distinctively unfamiliar yet sophisticated atmosphere.
At its heart stands ‘VESSEL’, a 7-meter concrete kinetic sculpture evoking
the sensation of floating through the cosmos—a bold expansion
of the conventional F&B experience.
