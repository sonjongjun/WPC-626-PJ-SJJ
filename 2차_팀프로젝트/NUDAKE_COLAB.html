COLLABORATIONS

  • NUDAKE TEAHOUSE


    The NUDAKE Teahouse is a space dedicated to our pioneering tea experiences. Here, you can savor ‘scented teas’ and carefully curated ‘dessert pairings,’ all created with audacious vision and understated sophistication. Through NUDAKE, we hope that your senses awaken, and the personal stories you thought forgotten quietly come to life.

    TEA

    At NUDAKE, we craft teas inspired by imagery beyond the ordinary, layering scents in surprising ways to create entirely new scented teas. At the Teahouse, explore twelve exquisite teas, served in elegant teapots and presented in thoughtfully designed packages.

  • WORLD ANIMAL DAY

    To commemorate 'World Animal Day' on October 4th, NUDAKE crafted a special cake for dogs. Celebrate with our exclusive images capturing the unique cake, space, and photoshoot experience!

    WITH GLAMOUR SHOT

    To mark 'World Animal Day', we partnered with the photo studio 'Glamorshot' for a family photoshoot. Explore the humorous and surreal atmosphere of the photoshoot below!

  • NUDAKE ♡ JENNIE

    'Nudake♡Jennie' is a special collaboration pop-up store presented by Nudake and Jennie. From four desserts celebrating Jennie's first solo album to a space that captures her various charms, visitors can experience new moments throughout the pop-up store.

    DESSERT

    Introducing four desserts created to embody Jennie's creativity. The four special desserts are: "Jennierubyjane," delicately finished with ribbon and ruby decorations; "Cameo," which features her intricately carved portrait; "Rouge Heel," a charming shoe-shaped creation; and "Cotta," offering the experience of two panna cotta flavors.

  • 2023 BIRTH

    “Why do we throw away birthday candles?”
    NUDAKE questions the fate of burnt-out candles, with their latest creation: ‘Birth’ cake, topped with edible, chocolate candles.

    SPACE

    To celebrate the release of the ‘Birth’, NUDAKE Seongsu adorned a spacious communal table with a "Birth" theme. Bread sculptures of animals and plants, symbolizing birth, added charm to the cozy setting, offering visitors a delightful visual treat.

  • 2021 PICNIC

    In the fall of 2021, NUDAKE launched a new collection focused on the theme of 'Dining'. This creative lineup shakes up cake ingredients, recipes, and how we eat them. It explores different ways of enjoying food beyond the usual meals and cakes.

    CONTEST

    The 'Picnic' cake can be versatile, depending on how candles are arranged or how the ingredients are presented. We hosted a contest to acknowledge individuals who creatively staged beautiful Picnic cakes, rewarding them with prizes.
